Comet

A comet on an elliptical orbit around the Sun. The ion tail (blue) points straight away from the Sun, pushed by the solar wind; the dust tail (orange) curves along the orbit because dust grains keep some of the comet's orbital motion. Both tails grow as the comet nears perihelion — activity scales roughly with 1/r².
